# ListenBrainz for Scrubbler

Send your scrobbles to ListenBrainz from Scrubbler. You can use it alongside Last.fm.

## Getting started

1. Open **Accounts** in Scrubbler and authenticate **ListenBrainz**.
2. Select **Open ListenBrainz settings**, copy your user token, and paste it into the dialog.
3. Select **Connect** and enable scrobbling for the account.

Your account stays connected between sessions. Scrobble using any of Scrubbler's scrobbling plugins.

Choose ListenBrainz as your account-functions provider to use now playing, love/unlove, play counts, tags, and music links in compatible plugins. Play counts may take time to update.
